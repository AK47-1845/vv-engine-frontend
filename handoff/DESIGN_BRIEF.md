# Design Brief

IN-PROGRESS: Finalize after the bounded reference inspection.

Concept: Instrument-grade trust. A dark physical-AI test instrument, not a generic AI landing page. Genuity Verify remains the product name from the existing project.

Planned palette: near-black, bright neutral text, one cyan signal accent, semantic green/pass and coral/fail. Evidence moments may use a light ground. Typography: self-hosted precise grotesk and mono; fixed responsive sizes, zero letter-spacing. Respect the higher-priority project design constraints instead of negative tracking or viewport-scaled text.

Planned sections: live verification hero, failure-injection demo, pinned four-stage pipeline, evidence/report view, regulatory timing, founder/build status/pilot ask, request-pilot dialog and explicitly unrecorded demo placeholder.

Signature interactions: shared robot trace plus streamed gates; deterministic failure injection with an explained verdict; one visual transforming through the evidence pipeline.

Claims: no assertion that the product proves complete physical safety, issues certificates, or has customers/pilots not supplied by the user.
