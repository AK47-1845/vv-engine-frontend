# Pitch Notes

## Three Seconds

The hero names the category: Physical AI, verified. The robot trace and explicit synthetic label show the product mechanism without implying deployed hardware or a safety certificate.

## Thirty Seconds

The problem is the gap between a capable model and a dependable system. The failure lab lets a buyer inject sensor dropout, perception conflict or distribution shift, then inspect the exact failed gate and HOLD decision. This is the primary memorable interaction.
